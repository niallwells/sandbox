# Halvorsen Resources — Voice & Tone

This file sets how we sound in writing. It covers ASX announcements, quarterly activities reports, shareholder emails, our website, investor materials, and every web story (Stori) published under the Halvorsen Resources (**ASX: HVR**) brand.

We write in one **corporate voice** (this file). Two **persona voices** sit on top of it:

- `voice-hvr-chairperson.md`: first-person text from our Non-Executive Chairperson.
- `voice-hvr-md.md`: first-person text from our Managing Director and CEO.

The persona files inherit everything here. They add register and habits, and they never loosen a rule. If a page carries a Chairperson block and an MD quote, each block follows its own file and the copy around them follows this one.

**Version 0.1.** Built from our own documents:
- the December 2025, March 2026 and June 2026 quarterly activities reports;
- the May 2026 MRE update;
- the MD's shareholder emails of August 2024 and August 2026;
- the FY2026 Chairperson's report;
- our short-form video Q&A set.

It pairs with `brand-guidelines-hvr.md`, which owns every number, date, currency and unit format used below.

> Halvorsen Resources, the Chilombo Copper Project and every person, partner and figure in this file are fictional. The example figures show format and register. They are not current and must not be reused as facts.

# Part 1 — Core craft

**Factual and precise.** Every claim is specific. A number carries its unit, its basis and its date. A resource looks like this: `170.2 Mt at 0.47% Cu (0.54% CuEq), 0.2% Cu cut-off, Indicated and Inferred, JORC 2012, as at 27 May 2026`. `Around 170 million tonnes` is not a resource.

**Sourced to a release.** Every fact about Halvorsen traces to one of our dated ASX announcements, quarterlies, annual reports or presentations. If we have not released it, it does not go in as fact.

**Active voice, one idea per sentence.** Write "Halvorsen reported…", not "It was reported that…". Our announcements run to long sentences. Web copy tightens them without changing the vocabulary or the claim.

**Correct term, then plain gloss.** Our readers range from institutional resource investors to retail holders. Use the right technical word (Indicated, up-dip, flotation, conductor), and give a lay reader enough context to follow it on first use.

**Honest.** No hedging theatre. No claim we have not stood behind in a release. No empty superlatives. When the market has asked a hard question, answer it directly.

**Australian English.** -ise, -our, programme, mineralisation, metres, tonnes. Dates as `29 June 2026`, financial years as `FY2026`, and quarters as `June 2026 quarter` in prose.

**No emoji, no exclamation marks.**

**Formatting follows `brand-guidelines-hvr.md`.**
- Units carry a space: `12 m`, `2 km`, `259 koz`, `805 kt`.
- Intercepts read `18 m at 3.2 g/t Au from 64 m (HOLE001)`.
- Currency is prefixed: `A$36 million` in prose, `A$36M` in tables.
- Every figure that can change carries `as at` and a date.

# Part 2 — Corporate voice (Halvorsen talking as itself)

### Register
We sound like a well-run explorer reporting what it did, what it found and what comes next. Confident, measured, technically grounded and forward-looking. We are Perth-based and ASX-listed, with one flagship asset in Zambia and a team that has done this before. A reader should finish any of our documents knowing three things: the resource grew, the project became technically safer, and we can pay for the next step.

### Audience
- Shareholders and interested readers, institutional and retail, in Australia and offshore.
- Our strategic partner.
- The Zambian stakeholders who read what we publish.

Announcement copy addresses the market. Emails and web stories address the reader.

### Principles

**Lead with the milestone, then the number, then what it means.** The Highlights pattern sets our rhythm: a bold label, a specific claim, then the consequence.

> **High Geological Confidence:** Approximately 36% of the Lwele Central MRE is within the Indicated category, providing a robust foundation for near-term development studies.

**Hold the three strands.** Most of our documents touch all three of these. A story that tells only one feels thin.
- Resource growth: drilling, MRE updates, regional targets.
- Technical de-risking: metallurgy, the Scoping Study, licences and infrastructure.
- Financial discipline: cash, zero debt, capital allocation.

**Use the fixed vocabulary.** Our names are part of the brand. Use the glossary below and do not invent variants.

**Confidence about our own project is allowed; verdicts about the market are not.** We say our grades are "directly comparable to world-class operating mines in the Zambian Copperbelt" because we released that comparison with its basis. Any piece may repeat a characterisation we have released, with a citation. It may not create a new one. We never say our shares are cheap or undervalued, or predict what the stock will do.

**Candour, then response.** Slow assay turnaround, a share price that lags the news flow, pending results: we name the constraint, give the cause and say what we are doing about it. We never leave a gap for the reader to fill.

**Momentum without hype.** Evaluative words are allowed when they attach to a specific released result:
- **We use:** significant, substantial, encouraging, compelling (of targets), exceptional (of metallurgical outcomes), strong, robust, well-funded, step-change.
- **We never use:** revolutionary, game-changing, transformational (of the share price), guaranteed, can't-miss, cheap, undervalued, re-rate, blue-sky, moonshot.

**"Unlock" and "de-risk" are our words.** We use "Unlocking the full potential of Chilombo" deliberately and often. We use it about the project, never about the share price.

### Document shapes

- **ASX announcement.**
  - A title-case headline that states the result, such as "Chilombo Resource Grows as Gold Inventory More Than Doubles".
  - HIGHLIGHTS bullets with bold labels.
  - The opening line "Halvorsen Resources Ltd (ASX: HVR) (Halvorsen) is pleased to announce…".
  - An MD comment block, then the body arranged by topic.
  - A Competent Person statement and a forward-looking caution.
  - Appendices for JORC Table 1 and the CuEq formula.
- **Quarterly activities report.** The same pattern, with sections for Highlights, MD comment, Project Development, Corporate, Appendix A (tenements) and Appendix B (CuEq), plus the Appendix 5B. Every quarter closes on the cash position: "Halvorsen well-funded to accelerate exploration and advancement of Chilombo with a robust cash balance of approximately A$30.5 million and zero debt as at 30 June 2026."
- **Shareholder email.** MD persona; see `voice-hvr-md.md`.
- **Chairperson's report / AGM address.** Chairperson persona; see `voice-hvr-chairperson.md`.
- **Short-form video Q&A.** MD persona, in its punchier mode; see `voice-hvr-md.md`.
- **Web story (Stori).** Built only from released material. The corporate voice carries the connecting copy, the persona files govern any first-person block, and Part 3 governs every claim.

### Required statements
These must be present and legible, and never collapsed. Placement rules are in `brand-guidelines-hvr.md`; the wording is ours.

- **Competent Person statements**, each with the consent sentence:
  - exploration results: our Consultant Geologist, Mr Graham Keeble (MAusIMM, SAIMM);
  - Mineral Resources and Exploration Targets: our independent Competent Person, Mr Michael Treloar (FAusIMM (CPGeo), Treloar Resource Geology);
  - metallurgical testing: our metallurgist (MAusIMM).
- **Previously reported information:** "Halvorsen confirms it is not aware of any new information or data which materially affects the information included in the original market announcements…"
- **Forward-looking caution:** "This announcement may contain some references to forecasts, estimates, assumptions, and other forward-looking statements. Although the Company believes that its expectations, estimates and forecast outcomes are based on reasonable assumptions, it can give no assurance that they will be achieved… Investors should make and rely upon their own enquiries before deciding to acquire or deal in the Company's securities."
- **CuEq methodology** whenever a CuEq figure appears. State the prices (Cu US$11,200/t, Au US$3,400/oz, Co US$38,000/t), the recoveries (Cu 88%, Co 55%, Au 68%) and the formula, and state that the elements have reasonable potential to be recovered and sold.
- **Classification and cut-off** on every resource figure. **An as-at date** on every cash figure.

# Part 3 — Mining (ASX) pack

> **Regime:** JORC Code 2012; ASX Listing Rules chapter 5 and continuous disclosure (chapter 3); the misleading-or-deceptive-conduct provisions of the Corporations Act. We are a listed company with retail readers, writing about our own project.

### North star
Anything we publish outside the ASX platform should read like a condensed version of our announcements. Every number is one we have released, every characterisation is one we have made, and nothing in it would surprise our Company Secretary. It persuades by being verifiable.

### The rule that defines the pack
**Nothing we publish is the first place a material fact appears.** Continuous disclosure means the ASX platform sees it first. If the source is marked draft, or a figure is still a placeholder ("A$X.X million", "XX July 2026"), it has not been released and it does not ship. Check the release date on the ASX announcements platform before citing anything.

### The four buckets

- **Bucket 1 — Thematic / commodity.** Copper demand from electrification and data-centre power, supply constraints, Zambia as a jurisdiction, regional investment flows. We have room to discuss outlook here. Numbers still need a source: "more than US$10 billion" of committed regional mining investment, from our Q&A set, needs its basis when it appears in print.
- **Bucket 2 — Sector / peer group, unnamed.** General statements only, with no implied ranking of specific entities.
- **Bucket 3a — Halvorsen about Halvorsen.**
  - We may carry characterisations we have released, each cited to its release. Examples: "directly comparable to world-class operating mines in the Zambian Copperbelt", "the largest drilling campaign undertaken at Chilombo to date". We may not extend them.
  - Every resource figure carries its classification, cut-off, code and date.
  - **No production targets, forecast financials, mine life, capital or operating cost figures, or NPV until a released JORC-compliant study supports them.** Until the Scoping Study is out, the only economic language we have is our own qualitative wording ("positive implications for future plant capital and operating costs").
- **Bucket 3b — Other named entities.** This covers Meridian Copper Corporation, Carrick Engineering, Lusaka Power Partners, Zambezi Minerals Development (ZMD, our 12.5% minority partner in Nkwazi Minerals), Summit Crest Holdings, Canaccord Genuity, and any peer or neighbour. Write about them strictly factually, with sources and no merit language. Describe each relationship exactly as we have announced it. Refer to neighbouring operations generically ("a major operating open-pit copper mine") unless a release of ours names them.
- **Bucket 4 — Analytical / AFSL.** Valuation, price targets, "undervalued", peer multiples, or anything implying buy, sell or hold. Never. The outer limit is the Chairperson's FY2026 remark that progress "has not been fully reflected in our share price performance", and only as a quoted, attributed and dated statement of the Board's view.

### Forward-looking language
We hedge the same way every time: "remains on track for", "targeted for completion", "is expected to", "has the potential to", "we believe". A date attached to a future event is always a target. Where a page collects several forward-looking statements, the forward-looking caution appears on that page.

### Pre-publish checklist
1. Is every fact traceable to a released, dated document? Is any placeholder or draft marking present?
2. Does every resource figure carry its classification, cut-off, code and date? Does every CuEq figure carry its methodology note?
3. Is every evaluative word one we have already used about that result, with a citation?
4. Is there any economic figure that a released study does not support?
5. Is any named entity other than Halvorsen described with merit language?
6. Could a reader take any sentence as a view on our share price or an invitation to buy?
7. Are the Competent Person statement and forward-looking caution present and legible where the page needs them?
8. Does every figure follow `brand-guidelines-hvr.md`?

If a sentence would survive being read aloud to our Company Secretary and our Competent Person without a flinch, it passes.
